Support and wellbeing

We offer care that goes beyond treatment

Peter Mac’s wellbeing approach is based on the belief that the best cancer care treats the person and the disease.

Practical, emotional and social support services are available for patients, families and carers.

Information and support can help carers look after themselves while caring for someone with cancer.
